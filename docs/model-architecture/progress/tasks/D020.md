## Scope

- Retain exact per-seed hit decisions for all-condition, four tag categories, Artist,
  candidate source, and each of the five NL branches.
- Record decisions independently of source presence and output overlap so hit rates are
  never inferred from an empty final body.
- Carry the fixed-key hits through `CaptionPlan`, `SerializedCaption`, and collate them
  into per-batch integer counts compatible with `TrainingMetric.dropout_hits`.

- No probability, RNG domain, caption content, deletion, NL selection, truncation, or

- The production 100,000-seed distribution run, final empty-body rate, and acceptance
  of `all_condition=10%+/-0.5pp` remain pending evidence.
- No GPU, DDP/NCCL, multi-GPU, long run, or formal stage evidence is claimed.
