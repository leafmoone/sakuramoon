Status: complete on one GPU; independent AI and Infra reviews passed.

## Scope

- Define the production trainable composite as exactly `dit`, `text`, and `style`; frozen Qwen and Mage-VAE remain outside its module tree, optimizer and checkpoint boundary.
- Accumulate each microbatch as the FP32 sum of its already per-sample-mean losses, count effective samples, and divide gradients once by the total sample count at the update boundary.
- Apply the T040 FP32 global clip at 1.0, then execute exactly one isolated TorchAO optimizer step and `zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`.
- Expose immutable attempted/successful update and effective-sample counters for the checkpoint boundary. A failed optimizer attempt cannot continue in place.
